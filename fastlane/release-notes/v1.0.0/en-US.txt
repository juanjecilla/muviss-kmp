Welcome to Muviss 1.0 — a private tracker for movies and TV shows.

• Tick episodes and Muviss works out what you're watching, what's next and what's coming.
• Fill your library fast: decide one title at a time, or snooze it for later.
• Watch next, upcoming episodes, new-episode alerts and a home-screen widget.
• Stats, rewatch history, ratings, notes and your own lists.
• Import from Trakt, TV Time or CSV.

No account, no ads, no social. Everything stays on your device.
