Muviss is a private tracker for movies and TV shows. Tick episodes as you watch them and Muviss works out the rest: what you're in the middle of, what's next, and what's coming.

TRACK WHAT YOU WATCH
• Search any movie or show, with posters, synopses, full episode guides and where to watch it.
• Tick episodes one by one, or a whole season of aired episodes at once.
• Automatic status: Not started, Watching, Watched or Finished, worked out from what you've actually seen and whether a show is still airing. You never set it by hand.
• Rewatch something? Every viewing is kept, not just the first.
• Rate titles, write yourself a note, mark favorites, and build your own lists.

KNOW WHAT'S NEXT
• Watch next: every show you're following, with its next unseen episode and a one-tap tick.
• Upcoming: air dates for your shows, day by day.
• New-episode notifications when something you follow airs.
• A home-screen widget so the next episode is one tap away.

FILL YOUR LIBRARY FAST
• Go through titles one at a time and decide in a tap — not for me, later, watching, or caught up. Not sure yet? Snooze it and it comes back when you choose.
• Import your history from Trakt, TV Time or a CSV file.
• Discover by genre, see what's popular, and get suggestions based on what you love.

SEE YOUR HABITS
• Hours watched, episodes seen, favorite genres and watch streaks.
• Your most rewatched shows and movies.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No sign-up, no login, nothing tied to your identity.
• No social features — no feeds, no friends, no comments.
• No ads and no analytics. Only optional, anonymous crash reports, which you can turn off in Settings.
• Your library lives on your device and works offline. Back it up to a file and restore it on any device.

In English and Spanish, with light and dark themes, on phones and tablets.
